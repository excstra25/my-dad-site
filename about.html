HIS STORY

A life built around learning and purpose.

Azuka Odanike Justice, born 1 March 1978, is a businessman with a passion for learning, reading and personal development.

Outside his professional life, Azuka enjoys playing football and spending time with the people closest to him. His interests reflect a belief that growth does not stop with formal education. Reading, learning from experience and remaining curious about the world are important parts of his life.

As a husband and father of four children, family occupies an important place in his life. His experiences as a businessman, husband and father provide a perspective on the importance of building safer and healthier communities for present and future generations.

Through this awareness project, Azuka seeks to encourage conversations around environmental responsibility, drug abuse prevention and the collective role individuals and communities can play in protecting one another.

Beyond his professional endeavors, Azuka has served as a community leader and political advocate, dedicating his efforts to humanitarian services. He has been at the forefront of seeking an egalitarian society where equal opportunities for development and growth are available to all members of the community.
